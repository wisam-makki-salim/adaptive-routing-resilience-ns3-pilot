# Relevance to Professor Peter Langendörfer

This pilot offers a concrete entry point into wireless and IoT resilience. It
measures when faster routing control helps, what radio-capacity cost it creates,
and why a resource-aware trigger must protect worst-flow service rather than
optimize aggregate delivery alone. The next step—guarded activation and exit
under explicit control-cost and tail-risk constraints—fits research on dependable,
resource-conscious networked systems while leaving the central controller-design
and validation work open for doctoral research.
